🚀 DEPLOY YOUR APP TO INTERNET - SUPER EASY METHOD
================================================

✅ Vercel is already installed on your computer!

Just follow these 3 simple steps:

STEP 1: Open a NEW Terminal/Command Prompt
-------------------------------------------
(Keep the development server running in the other terminal)


STEP 2: Navigate to Your Project
---------------------------------
cd "c:\Users\AC\Downloads\AI-Powered-System-Design-App-Starter-File-main\AI-Powered-System-Design-App-Starter-File-main"


STEP 3: Deploy with One Command
--------------------------------
vercel

That's it! Just type "vercel" and press Enter.

The Vercel CLI will:
1. Ask you to login (it will open your browser)
2. Ask a few simple questions (just press Enter to accept defaults)
3. Deploy your app to the internet!
4. Give you a live URL!

⏱️ Total time: 2-3 minutes

🌐 You'll get a URL like:
https://ai-system-design-app-abc123.vercel.app

Share that URL with anyone and they can use your app!

================================================

📝 IMPORTANT NOTES:

1. First time? You'll need to:
   - Create a Vercel account (free)
   - Login through your browser
   - Authorize Vercel CLI

2. Add your Gemini API key:
   After first deployment, go to:
   https://vercel.com/dashboard
   → Select your project
   → Settings
   → Environment Variables
   → Add: NEXT_PUBLIC_GEMINI_API_KEY = your_api_key
   → Redeploy

3. Future updates:
   - Make changes to your code
   - Run "vercel" again
   - Your app updates automatically!

================================================

🎉 THAT'S IT! Your app will be live on the internet!
