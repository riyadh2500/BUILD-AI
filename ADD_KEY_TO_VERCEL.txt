🔑 ADD YOUR OPENAI API KEY TO VERCEL

Your app is live at: https://ai-system-design-builder.vercel.app

To enable the AI Assistant, follow these steps:

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STEP 1: Go to Vercel Dashboard
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Open: https://vercel.com/ajmul-s-projects/ai-system-design-builder/settings/environment-variables


STEP 2: Add Environment Variable
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Click "Add New" button
2. Fill in the form:

   Variable Name:
   NEXT_PUBLIC_OPENAI_API_KEY

   Value:
   YOUR_OPENAI_API_KEY_HERE

   Environments:
   ✓ Production
   ✓ Preview
   ✓ Development

3. Click "Save"


STEP 3: Redeploy
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Go to: https://vercel.com/ajmul-s-projects/ai-system-design-builder
2. Click "Deployments" tab
3. Find the latest deployment
4. Click the three dots (•••) on the right
5. Click "Redeploy"
6. Wait ~1 minute for deployment to complete


STEP 4: Test It!
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Visit: https://ai-system-design-builder.vercel.app
2. Click the AI Assistant button (purple robot icon, bottom right)
3. Ask: "How to design a scalable ML system?"
4. GPT-4 should respond! 🎉
